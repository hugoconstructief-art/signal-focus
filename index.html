Signal & Focus

Ideas for curious minds.

Technology, AI & Science explained fast.

Signal & Focus creates original educational short-form videos about technology, AI and science. Each video explains one idea clearly in under a minute: how a padlock in your browser protects what you send, what a neural network is, why satellites do not fall straight down.

The publishing tool and TikTok

Signal & Focus uses a desktop publishing tool that runs on the creator's own computer. It helps the account owner move their own finished videos to TikTok. Specifically, the tool:

  • uses TikTok Login Kit, so the account owner signs in and approves access on TikTok's own pages;
  • uses the official TikTok Content Posting API;
  • uploads the account owner's own finished videos to TikTok;
  • currently uses a draft-first workflow: videos are sent to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts;
  • does not scrape TikTok;
  • does not use unofficial TikTok APIs.

Creator control

The creator decides what happens to every video. A video reaches TikTok only when an authorized upload is started, it arrives as a draft, and the creator reviews it, adds or edits the caption and chooses whether and when to publish it inside TikTok. Access can be revoked at any time from the creator's TikTok account settings.
